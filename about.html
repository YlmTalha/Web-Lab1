About This Website

This site exists so that drivers can see where they can charge and what it costs, and so that property owners can find out what it takes to have a charging point on their own site. The company has been operating its public network since 2023, and our aim is to make sustainable transport accessible to everyone.

“We are not only a charging station provider. We want to be part of a sustainable way of living.”

At a glance

  • Public locations: 34

How a Charging Session Works

How much does charging cost?

12.99 TL per kWh at DC stations and 9.99 TL per kWh at AC stations. You pay only for the energy you take.

Do I need the mobile application?

No. Units with a payment terminal accept a contactless bank card. The application is for finding stations and keeping your receipts together.
