Who We Are

Charge Mahal Enerji is an electric vehicle charging operator based in Istanbul. We install and run AC and DC charging stations for municipalities, shopping centres, petrol stations and residential sites, and we monitor every station from our own operations centre.

Our Charging Network

Our public network covers 34 locations across Ankara, Istanbul, Sakarya, Samsun, Sinop, Gaziantep and Denizli. Every station is open 24 hours a day and can be found through the Sarj Mahal mobile application.

Fast DC Charging

Our DC units charge two cars at once through dual CCS2 outputs and accept contactless payment. The public tariff is 12.99 TL per kWh for DC and 9.99 TL per kWh for AC.
